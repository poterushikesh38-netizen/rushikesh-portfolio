Safe Organizer Systems

Interior organizer systems for Godrej security safes — trays, dividers, and document racks configured to make better use of a fixed internal volume, taken from proof of concept through to a producible design.

Client
Godrej
Role
Design Engineer
Timeline
2023 — Present
Category
Product Design
🔐
POC → Prototype → Production

The Challenge

More usable storage inside a volume that can't grow

A safe's external dimensions are fixed by the security rating and door mechanism, so any improvement to how customers actually use the interior has to come from smarter organization — trays, tiered shelving, and document dividers — rather than from more space.

Approach

Iterating from proof of concept to a producible layout

I worked through several internal layout concepts against real customer use cases — cash, jewelry, documents — before settling on a configuration and pushing it through prototype validation and cost review.

  • Modeled multiple interior layout concepts in UG NX and Fusion 360 and benchmarked usable volume against the fixed safe envelope.
  • Built proof-of-concept prototypes to test tray strength, drawer travel, and how documents and valuables actually sat inside each configuration.
  • Selected sheet metal and coating specs for the organizer components based on cost and manufacturing lead time.
  • Carried the validated layout through to production-ready drawings and BOM handoff.
Outcome

A usable-space improvement without a bigger safe

The finalized organizer layout made significantly better use of the fixed interior volume, giving customers clearer, more accessible storage for documents and valuables without any change to the safe's external footprint.

Key Takeaway

"When the outer envelope is fixed, the design work moves entirely inside — every millimeter of layout has to earn its place."
