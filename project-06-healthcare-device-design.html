Modular ICU trolley system

A ground-up structural and enclosure redesign for a bedside medical trolley — balancing sterile-surface requirements, clinician ergonomics, and fast sheet-metal manufacturability.

Client
Healthcare OEM
Role
Design Engineer
Duration
10 Weeks
Tools
UG NX · FEA
🏥

The Challenge

Hospital-floor trolleys take constant impact and wipe-down cleaning cycles, so every joint, seam, and fastener had to resist corrosion and harbor no bacteria-trapping gaps. The existing frame was heavy, difficult to disinfect, and slow to assemble on the line.

The brief called for a lighter structure that kept rigidity under load, met IEC-relevant clearance rules for medical equipment, and could ship in modular sub-assemblies to cut logistics cost.

The Approach

I modeled the full trolley frame, shelving, and IV-pole mount in UG NX, iterating the sheet-metal folds to remove crevices and reduce part count. Structural FEA validated the frame against tipping and dynamic-load scenarios before any tooling was cut.

Close coordination with the powder-coating and welding vendors kept every bend radius and weld-access point within their process limits from the first release.

Process

01

Requirements & Benchmarking

Reviewed clinical workflows and competitor trolleys to set target weight, height, and storage criteria.

02

Structural Concepting

Explored frame topologies in UG NX, screening for part-count reduction and hygienic seam design.

03

Validation

Ran FEA for static and tip-load cases; adjusted gauge thickness and bracing to pass margins.

04

DFM Handoff

Finalized BOM, laser-cut nesting, and weld-fixture drawings for pilot-batch production.
