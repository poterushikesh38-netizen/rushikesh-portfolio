Corporate memento & award design

A family of desk mementos and recognition awards, engineered in metal and wood for consistent finish quality across small-batch, high-detail production runs.

Client
Corporate Gifting
Role
Design Engineer
Duration
6 Weeks
Tools
Fusion 360 · AutoCAD
🏆

The Challenge

Mementos live or die on finish quality — a single visible tool mark or misaligned engraving ruins the piece. The brief needed a design that looked hand-crafted but could be laser-cut, bent, and polished repeatably across a run of a few hundred units, each personalized with a name plate.

Material cost also had to stay tight, since these were gifted in volume for an annual recognition event rather than sold individually.

The Approach

I modeled each memento variant in Fusion 360, designing the base, riser, and name-plate as separate laser-cut components that self-locate during assembly — removing the need for jigs. Bend and polish tolerances were set against what the vendor's finishing line could hold consistently.

A shared base geometry across the award tiers let the client reuse tooling while still differentiating the top pieces by rank.

Process

CAD Modeling

03

04

Batch Production

Released nesting layouts and assembly drawings for the personalized production run.
