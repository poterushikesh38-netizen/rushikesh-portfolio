Home appliance enclosure design

A full sheet-metal enclosure redesign for a mid-size home appliance, cutting part count and assembly time while holding the client's existing brand surface language.

Client
Appliances OEM
Role
Design Engineer
Duration
8 Weeks
Tools
UG NX · AutoCAD
🧺

The Challenge

The existing enclosure used more brackets and fasteners than the assembly line needed, adding time and cost to every unit built. The client wanted the redesign to hold the same external look and vent performance while trimming part count and simplifying the fold sequence.

Thermal clearance around the internal components also had to be preserved exactly, since any change risked re-certification.

The Approach

I re-modeled the enclosure in UG NX as fewer, larger sheet-metal panels with integrated snap and hem features, replacing several bracket-and-fastener joints. Each fold sequence was checked against the press-brake's tooling limits before release.

Vent geometry and internal clearances were carried over from the validated design and rechecked against the new panel layout to avoid re-testing the thermal path.

Process

01

Teardown & Audit

Mapped every fastener and bracket in the existing enclosure to find consolidation opportunities.

02

Panel Redesign

Modeled consolidated panels with integrated snap and hem joints in UG NX.

03

Tooling Check

Verified every bend against the press-brake's tonnage and radius limits.

04

Pilot Build

Assembled and timed a pilot run to confirm the assembly-time savings.
